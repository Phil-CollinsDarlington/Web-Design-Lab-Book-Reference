Welcome to the Department

The Department of Information Technology at ISBAT University provides learning in computing, information systems and modern digital technologies. Students develop problem-solving, communication and technical skills.

Through classroom learning and practical activities, the department prepares learners to apply technology to real-world challenges. It encourages innovation, teamwork and responsible use of information technology.

Programmes Offered

  • Bachelor of Science in Applied Information Technology
  • Bachelor of Science in Computer Science
  • Diploma in Information Technology
  • Certificate in Information Technology
